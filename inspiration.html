What the site takes from others

References the designer picked for her portfolio site, what to borrow from each, and what each needs to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Mirrored in Notion: For Portfolio.

Decisions

  • D1The site is white. Every page, in every case.
  • D2Every project opens as its own page.
  • D4Each project shows its name, a short description, my role, a large image and a way to open it.
  • D5Previews are large images on a background, not device mockups.
  • D6About: photo, bio, dated journey, personal qualities, and testimonials with name and contacts.
  • D7Resume opens as a classic white PDF.
  • D8Typeface close to Aeonik Regular or Neue Haas Grotesk.
  • D9Hero: a large headline with animated interface elements drawn in outline.

Standards check, WCAG 2.2 AA

Every pattern above can be built to conform. This is what each one needs.

PatternCriterionRequirementStatus
White site1.4.3 ContrastText ≥ 4.5:1 on white. Lightest passing grey: #767676 (4.54:1).Conforms if greys stay at or below #767676.
Animated hero2.2.2 Pause, Stop, HideAuto-motion over 5 s needs a visible pause.Pause button, or motion that stops within 5 s.
prefers-reduced-motionHonour the OS setting (2.3.3 is AAA, advisory).Plan a still version.
1.4.3Small blue labels need 4.5:1.Measure once the blue is chosen.
Image previews1.1.1 Non-text contentAlt text naming the project and what is shown.Content task.
“View project”2.4.4 Link purposeUnique accessible names, e.g. “View AnyBiz project”.Add hidden text or aria-label.
2.5.8 Target size≥ 24 × 24 px.Conforms at reference sizes.
TestimonialsPrivacy / GDPRConsent to publish each person’s name and contact.Ask each referee.
Resume PDF1.3.1, PDF/UATagged PDF with real text.Export setting.
TypefaceLicenceAeonik and Neue Haas Grotesk need a paid web licence. Free open-licence grotesks: Inter Tight, Geist, Hanken Grotesk.Open.
