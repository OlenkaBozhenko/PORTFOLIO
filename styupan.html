Brand mark

A mark for an Oslo translation studio.

Role
Logo, brand identity
Year
2023 – 2024
Client
Stiupan Translations ENK, Oslo
Typeface
Crimson Text

About

Stiupan Translations is a multilingual translation studio in Oslo. The small team translates, proofreads, localises and writes copy in Norwegian, English, Ukrainian, Russian, German and Polish, under the line “Lost words found here”.

Objective

A mark that says “language and text” at a glance, reads at favicon size and in print, and works in one colour as well as two.

Idea

The initials S and T sit on the two pages of an open book. The left page is solid and the S is cut out of it; the right page is open and carries the T. Because the S is always the colour of the ground, the mark swaps cleanly between light and dark.
