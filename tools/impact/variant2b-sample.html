My impact

  1. 01

    AI email optimization, shipped as an MVP

    The business wanted far more than engineering could build in time. I interviewed users to find what mattered most, and we agreed on an MVP that worked for users, the business and engineering.

  2. 02

    Charts people actually understand

    Feedback showed that good-looking charts did not mean users followed the logic behind them. I rebuilt the dashboards around the metrics users rely on.

  3. 03

    20% more sales time for buyers

    Many booked sales calls turned out to be support questions. I redesigned the booking form: it shows what the call will cover and lets people switch to the right consultant. Sales got 20% more time for customers ready to buy.

  4. 04

    Several audiences, not one

    A customer journey map and an audit of product analytics showed that Warmy serves several distinct groups, each relying on different features. Every team got a clear picture of how to work with each group.
