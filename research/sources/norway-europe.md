## 4. Work format, visas, remote from Ukraine
- Sample of 16: hybrid ~9, fully remote ~4, onsite / unstated ~3.
- European startups cut fully remote listings ~35% year on year; hybrid 2–3 days is the default (Flexa via Sifted, May 2026 — https://remoteworkeurope.eu/news/2026/sifted-european-startups-remote-roles-drop-35-percent/).
- UK: 4 of 41 permanent senior PD ads remote.
- Norway: Oslo-based, onsite / hybrid; ~2 of 5 senior ads require Norwegian.

Norway: collective protection in one-year renewals; proposed route to a work permit ([Envoy](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/norway-proposes-new-work-based-residence-permit-for-ukrainians/)).
- **EU-remote roles** exist (Finom, Jimdo, Reedsy) but are a minority with more applicants.
- **Poland B2B (JDG)** needs Polish business registration → in practice residence; 2026 reforms raise reclassification risk ([Multiplier](https://www.usemultiplier.com/poland/hiring-contractors)).
- **Staying in Ukraine:** EOR or direct contractor agreement ([Employsome](https://employsome.com/employer-of-record/best-ukraine-eor/)).
- **Norway:** a foreign employer without a Norwegian entity cannot simply employ remotely in Norway ([L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/countries/norway/norway-remote-work-faq/)).

## 5. What European hiring managers want
- **Tom Scott**, Verified Insider, 2026-02-19 — https://verifiedinsider.substack.com/p/faq-design-hiring-in-2026 : lead with outcomes; 3–5 cases; interactive prototypes; most recent work; AI fluency; player-coach seniors.
- **Fountain Institute** 2026 — https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/blog/senior-product-designers-hired : shipped over concept; judgment over process diagrams; depth in one problem space; live Figma problem-solving in round one.
- **Micka Touillaud**, Feedly, 2024-11 — https://micka.substack.com/p/what-really-matters-when-hiring-product : ~70% fail the visual first impression; red flags: distant mockups, template portfolios on free domains, slow prototypes.
- Postings: Polarsteps "typography, motion, layout, storytelling, not just UX flows"; DataSnipper "the process to the end result"; Humaans craft vs Linear / Notion; Six Robotics "complex, interactive systems"; Finom async screen → take-home → panel.
