## 4. Work format
Robert Half Q2 2026 US postings ([read](https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/insights/research/remote-work-statistics-and-trends)): all — 87% onsite, 10% hybrid, 3% remote; technology — 85 / 11 / 4; marketing & creative — 82 / 14 / 4. Fully onsite rose from 65% (Q4 2025) [snippet].
Senior PD sample: ~8 remote-US, ~8 hybrid (usually 3 days), ~2 onsite. **[est]** In tech ~35–40% of senior design roles are remote. RTO tightening at large / traditional employers; remote-first software companies hold.

## 5. What US hiring managers want
- [NN/g State of UX 2026](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/state-of-ux-2026/): avoid "design theater"; show user problems solved to reach business goals.
- [UX Playbook](https://uxplaybook.org/articles/senior-ux-designer-portfolio-get-hired-2026): quantified business value, strategic context, leadership, ownership through launch, executive-ready storytelling; 6–8 s first scan.
- [Tom Scott](https://verifiedinsider.substack.com/p/faq-design-hiring-in-2026): most recent work, 3–5 cases, interactive prototypes, AI fluency, "owning more of the frontend".
- [Fountain Institute](https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/blog/senior-product-designers-hired) (2026-06-10): live 25-min Figma problem in the first interview; Loom walkthroughs; 3–5 cases. Its 73% / 79% / 56% AI figures are second-hand, unverified.
- [Opendoors Careers](https://blog.opendoorscareers.com/p/the-ultimate-portfolio-guide-for-product-designers-in-2026) (2026-04-01): position for one segment; each case scannable in 30–60 s; motion / prototype clips; vibe-coded portfolios are acceptable.

Caveats: 21 tech postings over-represent startups and fintech; non-tech tier thinly covered.
