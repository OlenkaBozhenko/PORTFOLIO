What every market asks for

Experience

5+ years, end to end

Owning a product area from discovery to release, not a single feature.

Most requested

Design systems

13 of 15 Ukrainian ads, about 17 of 20 European ones. Figma at token and variable level.

New in 2026

AI you can show

Working prototypes in Cursor or Claude Code. Designer Fund: half of designers have shipped AI-generated code.

Evidence

Metrics and impact

19 of 21 US postings. Experiments, funnels, conversion.

Soft skill #1

Defending decisions

Explaining trade-offs to PMs, engineers and stakeholders.

Regulated domains

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 AA in health and fintech; 29% of UK ads.

What recruiters want in a senior portfolio

Number of cases3–5 focused cases (NN/g, Tom Scott). Figma’s recruiter: 2–3 end to end.
Case structureProblem → your role and team → how solutions developed → the solution → rejected concepts and why → user and business impact → lessons (NN/g, 204 hiring managers).
Time spent~10 s on the homepage, then 2–3 min per case if it earns it.
OrderOutcome first, process second. Most recent work over most polished old work.
Seniority signalsInitiative (co-writing the brief), product thinking, scope across teams, trade-offs under uncertainty, influence, mentoring.
AI signalInteractive prototypes, a case about how you changed the team’s workflow, short video walkthroughs.
HomepageOne line on who you are and what you do; cases one click away; Work / About / Resume / Contact.
NDA workShow artefacts, redact, or rebuild generically. Give the password in the application. A fully locked site is a red flag.
The site itselfMobile layout, speed, HTTPS, WCAG 2.2 AA: one H1, keyboard access, visible focus, 4.5:1 contrast, alt text.

Red flags: “we” everywhere with no personal role · process diagrams without outcomes · no shipped work · a template more impressive than the work · long text · heavy animation · typos and broken links · a Senior title with feature-level scope.
