About

I'm a senior product designer in Oslo. Since 2021 I've designed B2B SaaS for sales outreach and email deliverability at AnyBiz and Warmy: design systems, prototypes, user interviews, research and analytics, more and more of it with AI tools.

Before design I spent seven years in law. It taught me to read the fine print, write things down precisely and stand behind a decision.
