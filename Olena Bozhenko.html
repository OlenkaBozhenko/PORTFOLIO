About

I'm a senior product designer. Since 2021 I've designed B2B and B2C products: SaaS for sales outreach and email deliverability at AnyBiz and Warmy, and a crowdfunding platform for Sport Guardian. My work covers design systems, prototypes, user interviews, research and analytics, more and more of it with AI tools.

Before design I spent seven years in law. It taught me to read the fine print, write things down precisely and stand behind a decision.

  • Keeps learning: courses and certificates every year
  • Goes to design and AI conferences
  • Works with Ukrainian, Israeli, Norwegian and American companies
